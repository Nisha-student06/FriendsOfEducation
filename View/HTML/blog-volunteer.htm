Volunteer Story

Posted on Aug 15, 2025 | Category: Volunteer Experience

Meet Raj, one of our dedicated volunteers who spent his summer teaching at our community centers. His story is a reflection of how small efforts can make a big difference in the lives of children.

Raj joined Friends of Education with the hope of giving back to society. Over the course of two months, he worked with students from rural backgrounds, focusing on English and basic computer literacy. His sessions were interactive and filled with activities that encouraged participation and creativity.

“I was amazed by the eagerness of these children to learn,” Raj shares. “Despite limited resources, their curiosity and determination were boundless. It inspired me to give my best every single day.”

“Volunteering here has been life-changing. I didn’t just teach, I also learned – patience, compassion, and the real meaning of community.”

Raj’s story reminds us that volunteering is not just about giving—it’s about receiving too. Every interaction creates bonds, spreads joy, and builds stronger communities.

If you would like to create your own story with us, consider joining our volunteer program. Together, we can continue shaping brighter futures.

Community Drive

Posted on Sept 1, 2025 | Category: Community Outreach

Last month, Friends of Education organized a massive Community Drive to bring together volunteers, parents, and local leaders in support of education. The event was a vibrant example of how collective action can empower entire neighborhoods.

More than 300 people joined us for a day filled with learning activities, storytelling sessions, and free distribution of school kits. Children participated in drawing competitions and interactive games designed to boost creativity and confidence.

The highlight of the drive was a parent-teacher interaction where parents learned simple techniques to support their children’s studies at home. It was a reminder that education is a partnership between schools, NGOs, and families.

“When communities unite for education, the impact is multiplied – children feel supported both in and out of classrooms.”

We thank all our volunteers and supporters who made this event possible. Your contributions—whether time, resources, or encouragement—helped us spread the light of education even further.

Stay tuned for our upcoming events and join us in our journey to build stronger, educated communities.

Education for All

Posted on Aug 10, 2025 | Category: Awareness

Education is not a privilege but a fundamental right. At Friends of Education, we firmly believe that every child, regardless of their socio-economic background, deserves access to quality education.

In our recent drive, we supported over 100 students by distributing school kits including bags, books, stationery, and uniforms. These resources not only motivate children to attend school but also reduce the financial burden on their families.

Our volunteers visited government schools in rural areas, interacting with children, parents, and teachers. The excitement on the faces of the students when receiving their kits was priceless.

Through such initiatives, we aim to create a strong foundation for lifelong learning, break the cycle of poverty, and empower the next generation.

“When you educate one child, you change an entire family. When you educate many, you transform a community.”

We encourage you to support our mission by contributing or becoming a volunteer. Together, we can truly achieve Education for All.

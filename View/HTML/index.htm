About Friends of Education

Friends of Education (FOE) is a non-profit organization dedicated to helping communities grow with dignity. Our vision is not limited to education alone—we extend our support to healthcare, food distribution, women empowerment, and social welfare initiatives.

We believe that every individual deserves equal opportunities to learn, live, and lead a better life. By joining hands with communities, volunteers, and donors, FOE has been able to touch many lives and build hope for a brighter tomorrow.

Our Vision & Mission
